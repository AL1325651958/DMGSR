# Findings: confirmatory run, mechanism ladder, and an archive inconsistency

Written after executing the runs in this session. Everything here is a measured
result or a verified file-level fact, not an inference from reading code.

## 1. The archived diffusion ensemble is stale, and it explains several numbers

`outputs/masked_v9_graph/masked_ensemble_validation.npz` was **not** produced by
the current sampler. The evidence:

| Quantity | Archived file | Current code, same checkpoint and same masks | Manuscript Table 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2011 RMSE | 68.94 | 24.41 | 24.51 |
| 2011 CRPS | 38.07 | 14.22 | 14.46 |
| Mean prediction | 171.51 | 135.17 | — |
| Truth mean | 135.17 | 135.17 | — |

Direct test: re-running the sampler with the pre-fix 31-day energy target
(`legacy bounded_partial_project`) reproduces the archived file's numbers to the
second decimal — **rmse 69.01, pred_mean 171.51** against the archive's
**68.94 / 171.51**. The current code gives 24.41 / 135.17. The archive therefore
predates the valid-day-count fix, and its hidden cells are systematically
inflated by the padded-length energy target.

Files that consume that archive and are consequently affected:

- `outputs/masked_v9_graph_calibrated/*` — the calibration was fitted to a
  stale, badly biased ensemble (its raw reporting CRPS 38.07 is the archive's).
- `outputs/graph_retraining_verification/*` — its diffusion RMSE 72.56 matches
  the stale ensemble's score, but its U-Net comparator is reported as 22.76,
  which the stale diffusion cannot be the source of. This directory's two
  branches are **not** mutually consistent and it should not be cited until it
  is regenerated.

What is **not** affected: `paper/evidence/final_ensembles.npz` reproduces
Table 1 exactly (Diffusion 24.514, UNet 20.839, Fusion 19.767), and it is the
file the manuscript's numbers come from. The published headline is sound.

Recommended action: regenerate those two output directories from the current
code before they are cited anywhere.

## 2. A retraction of my own earlier claim

In the previous round I reported that "the archived ensemble scores 72.56 while
Table 1 reports 24.51" and raised it as a possible reproducibility failure of the
manuscript. That framing was wrong in one respect: the 24.51 is fully
reproducible. I re-ran the sampler on the stored 2011 masks under twelve
independent seeds and obtained RMSE **24.21 ± 0.30** (range 23.64–24.75) and CRPS
**14.19 ± 0.21**, with both batching orders tested. `paper/evidence/
final_ensembles.npz` also reproduces the table directly.

An intermediate run of mine reported 70.49 for the fresh sampler; that was a bug
in my own `reproduce_2011.py`, where the month index array was built from the
global key index but then used to index a list, so the sampler ran on the wrong
months. The module is fixed and now reports 24.41. I record this because the
false alarm was mine, not the manuscript's.

## 3. Confirmatory run on the untouched 2012–2014 period

Protocol fixed in advance in `PREREGISTRATION_2012_2014.md`. Frozen weights, no
training, no seed search; calibration on 2010 only (2011 excluded because it
selected the checkpoint); evaluation on all 36 months of 2012–2014.

Calibration selected `w = 0.40, γ = 3.50` for the fusion and `γ_u = 2.00` for the
U-Net comparator (on the published 2010–2011 period it selected `w = 0.80,
γ = 4.25`).

| Decision rule | Outcome |
|---|---|
| C1 point accuracy | **confirmed** — fusion beats calibrated U-Net by 10.65 W/m² (24.0%), paired bootstrap 95% [−14.61, −7.31] |
| C2 reliability | **confirmed** — fusion coverage 90.7%, inside [0.87, 0.93] |
| C3 not only widening | **confirmed** — fusion CRPS 19.43 vs 26.35 |
| C4 underdispersion replicates | **confirmed** — raw coverage 63.0% (diffusion), 49.5% (U-Net), both < 0.70 |

Per seed on the confirmatory period, fusion RMSE 33.70 / 31.17 / 32.94 against a
calibrated-U-Net RMSE of 44.35.

**Two caveats that must travel with this result.**

1. The confirmatory masks hide 6.67% of valid cells, while the manuscript's
   stored mask hides **1.32%**. The manuscript's mask is far lighter than its own
   Methods description implies. Absolute scores are therefore not comparable
   across the two protocols; the confirmatory run is a *harder* task.
2. The U-Net scores 44.7 on my 2010 masks and 44.3 on my 2012–2014 masks, so the
   confirmatory period is not intrinsically harder than the calibration period.
   The 44.35 figure is a property of the mask regime, not of the years.

## 4. The sampling mechanism is inert

`outputs/mechanism_ladder/ladder_validation.json`, frozen checkpoint, one axis
varied at a time, 12 members, 2 676 hidden cells.

| chunk_size | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 16 | 32 | 64 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| RMSE | 33.213 | 33.214 | 33.215 | 33.214 | 33.214 | 33.215 | 33.215 |
| CRPS | 19.400 | 19.400 | 19.400 | 19.400 | 19.400 | 19.400 | 19.400 |
| coverage | 0.667 | 0.667 | 0.667 | 0.667 | 0.667 | 0.667 | 0.667 |

Applying the correction every single reverse step (chunk = 1) and once at the end
(chunk = 64) give the same answer to three decimals. Likewise:

- projection timing: per-chunk 33.214 vs final-only 33.176 (a 0.11% difference);
- gain form: adaptive 33.214 vs uniform 33.177 vs full 33.178.

Combined with the graph ablation (four configurations indistinguishable), the
entire sampling-time correction machinery — graph boundary update, variance
adaptive gain, chunked monthly projection and its feedback — has no measurable
effect on the reported metrics. The final processing pass evidently re-imposes
the outcome regardless of the trajectory taken to reach it.

This is a substantive negative result about the method the paper proposes. It
should be reported rather than omitted, and it means the manuscript's framing
cannot rest on the correction sequence.
